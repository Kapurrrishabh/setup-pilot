# Code of conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and level, criticise code and
not people, and accept feedback in good faith. Harassment of any kind is not allowed.

If someone breaks these rules, tell the maintainers privately through
[GitHub's private report form](https://github.com/Kapurrrishabh/setup-pilot/security/advisories/new)
and start the title with "Conduct:". Reports are kept private, and the maintainers may warn,
suspend or remove anyone who breaks the rules.
